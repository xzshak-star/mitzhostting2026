Help Center

Quick answers.

Token issues

Tokens only come from our Telegram bot (/start). They are permanent. If you lose it, generate a new one with /token (new free slot).

Bot won't start

  • Open Manage → Logs for the exact error
  • Make sure a .py entry exists
  • Dependencies: put a requirements.txt — install is manual on free tier for now

Bot limit

Free accounts can host 1 bot. Delete the old one to deploy a new one, or get premium from admin.

Banned?

Contact admin. Ban is by hosting token.
